Where long distances cluster

Getis-Ord Gi* on referral distance (10 km neighbourhood, 95% confidence, false discovery rate correction) finds 924 settlements in significant hot spots (average about 17 km to a referral hospital) and 1,449 in cold spots (about 6 km); 614 are not significant.

What this means

Primary clinics reach almost every settlement, but a patient needing surgery, emergency obstetric care or specialist treatment faces a median trip of 9.4 km, and about 45% of settlements are beyond 10 km. Investment is better aimed at upgrading selected primary centres into secondary hospitals in the hot spot LGAs than at adding more clinics.

Method and limits

Straight-line distance from 2,987 GRID3 settlement points inside the Ondo boundary (18 GRID3 LGA polygons dissolved) to the nearest facility in the GRID3 National Health Facility Registry (2024, all of Nigeria, so neighbouring-state hospitals count). Roads and rivers are not modelled, so real travel is longer. Settlements are counted, not people. Results depend on registry completeness and level labels.
